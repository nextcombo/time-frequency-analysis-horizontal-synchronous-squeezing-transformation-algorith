# Time-Frequency Analysis: Horizontal Synchronous Squeezing Transformation: Algorithms and Applications [with MATLAB Code]

Over the past few decades, the advancements in synchronous compression transform (SCT) as a post-processing time-frequency method for non-stationary signal analysis have received significant attention. Numerous studies have focused on improving the accuracy of estimated instantaneous frequencies (IF). In certain domains, the IF spectrum of signals exhibits rapid changes, even parallel to the frequency axis. Therefore, most existing methods may not be fully capable of handling transient signals such as guided waves and vibrational waves. To address this issue, a new method, called Horizontal Synchronous Compression Transform (HST), is introduced in this study. By applying local estimation of group delay (GD), the proposed technique can produce a compact time-frequency representation of transient signals while maintaining their reversibility. For validation purposes, the proposed method was implemented both synthetically and in real signals. The results convincingly show smaller Raney entropy values and improved reconstruction accuracy. HST is considered a reliable and accurate method for transient signal analysis with broad practical applications.
